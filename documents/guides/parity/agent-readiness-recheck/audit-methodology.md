# Is Agentic methodology

Is Agentic estimates how readily AI agents can discover, access, understand, and use a public website. Every check is run and scored by Ora. Is Agentic groups those checks by maturity and applicability to produce the score shown here, so checks that do not apply to a site do not count against it.

## Scoring model

Essential checks share an 80-point pool and Recommended checks share a 20-point pool. Emerging signals can add a small bonus, capped at five points, without becoming requirements. Not-applicable checks are excluded, partial results receive proportional credit, and duplicated check IDs across MCP surfaces are averaged.

## Data source

Ora runs the scan and scores each layer, check, and recommendation. Is Agentic organizes those results into Essential, Recommended, and Bonus groups and adds a prioritized implementation prompt, observed agent journeys, public share pages, caching, and historical storage.

## Freshness and limitations

Completed reports are cached for fast retrieval and may change when the target, Ora methodology, or time-sensitive evidence changes. A score is not a security, accessibility, quality, compliance, or compatibility certification. Automated checks can produce partial or incorrect results, so review the individual evidence in context.
